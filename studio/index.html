?A few helpful thingsControls, clues and round rules.+

Move: hold and drag on the maze. A keyboard works too: arrow keys or WASD.

Your mission: collect all three gold relics, then return to the blue portal.

Read the monsters: ! means chasing; ? means searching.

Sharp trackers: they always know where you are, even through walls. Try the other ideas if you want hiding around corners to help.

Caught? Your relics stay collected. A catch that brings all three relics home counts as rescued. Escaping means walking home yourself.

Round rules: practice lets you keep trying. In the three-life trial, your third catch ends the attempt, even if all three relics are collected.

Take a breath: pause whenever you like. Switching away from this page pauses the round.

Explore safely: learn your routes without a chase.

These are test rules you can change. A low catch count alone cannot tell you which game is the most fun.
